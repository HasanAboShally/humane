# Version example: what changed?

This is an authored illustration with fictional inputs, not legal advice or a recorded model result.

**Request:** “Use Humane to compare these versions. What changed, and what should I not miss?”

## Example response

The monthly fee decreases from $120 to $100, excluding tax. The twelve-month minimum remains. Calculated over twelve months, that is $1,200 rather than $1,440 before tax: $240 less, not an additional annual-payment discount. ([Previous fees](previous.md#fees), [amended fees](current.md#fees))

**Do not miss the cancellation condition:** 30 days' notice is required before the minimum term ends; leaving earlier still requires the remaining monthly fees. The amendment explicitly replaces only the Fees section, so omission of cancellation text does not remove that obligation. ([Cancellation](previous.md#cancellation), [amendment scope](current.md#scope-of-amendment))

Coverage: both supplied versions read. This explains those excerpts; review the applicable full agreement before relying on it.

## What the reader should understand

The comparison uses an explicit supersession statement, not just a date. Computed amounts are labeled, and the unchanged condition stays beside the attractive price change.
