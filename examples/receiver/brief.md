# Receiver example: three files, one brief

This is an authored illustration with fictional inputs, not a recorded model result.

**Request:** “Use Humane on the proposal, finance note, and status update. I'm reviewing the pilot. What needs my attention?”

## Example response

The two-week pilot for 20 volunteers is still a proposal. **The cost is unresolved:** the project plan estimates $8,000 and finance estimates $10,000 for the same scope, both excluding tax and the venue deposit. Neither is an approved budget. ([Plan](plan.md#budget), [Finance](finance.md#pilot-estimate), [deposit](finance.md#approval-constraint))

- **Clarify the budget:** finance requires the final supplier quote before approval; the quote is pending and the deposit amount is unknown. ([Finance](finance.md#approval-constraint), [Plan](plan.md#budget))
- **Check the schedule:** the coordinator requests an answer by 17 September 2026. The proposed 21 September start still depends on a venue that is not confirmed. ([Status](status.md#venue-and-schedule))
- **Keep the test modest:** success means at least 16 of 20 volunteers complete signup without help. It does not establish readiness for a public rollout. Twelve people have expressed interest, but none has enrolled. ([Plan](plan.md#success-measure), [Status](status.md#volunteers))

**Suggested next step:** ask for a reconciled quote, deposit amount, and venue confirmation before treating cost or start date as settled.

Coverage: all three supplied files read. No supplier quote was supplied.

## What the reader should understand

The budget disagreement has not been resolved by picking the newer document. Dates and participant counts retain their conditions. The proposed follow-up is visibly a suggestion, not an action taken. A reader can go directly to the underlying passages.
