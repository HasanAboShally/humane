# More useful, not more complicated

## The person we are helping

A person has received several documents and needs to understand them before a conversation or decision. Or they are preparing material that someone else will have to read. The useful result is accurate understanding with less reconstruction, not just a low word count.

## Decisions for the private preview

**One skill, no modes to learn.** Infer whether the person wants a brief, a draft, or a comparison from the request. Do not require sender tags, a settings file, a profile, or a special filename convention.

**Do the work between the documents.** Remove duplication, distinguish different scopes from genuine contradictions, and preserve unresolved disagreement. Repetition is not corroboration. Newer does not automatically mean authoritative.

**Make the next check obvious.** Surface consequential uncertainty and point to the relevant passage. Explain why a request or deadline matters to the stated task. Do not pretend to decide the reader's priorities without context.

**Use a short first layer.** Begin with the answer and necessary caveats. Do not append a full second report, long source inventory, or ceremonial quality checklist unless it helps. Keep deeper explanation available through references and follow-up.

**Prevent misleading compression.** Preserve differences between a suggestion and a commitment, interest and enrollment, an estimate and approval, an omitted clause and its cancellation. Catch arithmetic or unit mismatches rather than polishing them.

**Let the same words work in either direction.** “Use Humane on this” should help the receiver understand; “make this humane for the reader” should help the sender prepare. Neither requires proving that AI generated the content.

## What we are not adding

No backend, browser extension, document converter, automatic inbox access, hosted upload endpoint, tracking, scoring badge, bespoke installer, mandatory tags, or extra agent commands. No persistent reader profile without consent. No claim of local processing merely because instructions are Markdown.

A website and a new instruction-file convention can wait. The domain has not been purchased or configured by this work. The repository must stay private until the owner explicitly approves publication.

## What “10x” means here

It is an ambition, not a result. A five-minute read that hides the one important condition is worse than a fifteen-minute read that exposes it. We will measure whether a person understands and checks the work with less effort before claiming any improvement.

The current skill's safeguards are instructions to a model, not enforcement. Static packaging tests, authored examples, and model smoke tests have separate purposes; none proves human benefit.

## Small human-reader pilot

Use fictional material first or obtain explicit permission to use and share real material. Recruit 5–8 intended readers for an exploratory pilot, not a statistically conclusive claim.

Prepare several comparable document sets and establish, from the sources, an answer key: main point, requested decision if any, key numbers, important condition, unresolved conflict, and passage to check. Keep that answer key hidden from the generating assistant and the reader until scoring.

Compare ordinary prompting, an existing writing/summarizing approach, and Humane using the same model, source inputs, and requested length. Record exact prompts, model/client version, date, and output. Do not cherry-pick only Humane's best attempt. A separate researcher should anonymize the outputs so readers do not know which approach produced them.

Randomize and counterbalance assignments. Do not show the same source set to one reader repeatedly: remembering the first version would confound later review times. Include a source-only condition on separate matched sets if the question is whether a brief helps at all.

Ask readers to explain in their own words what matters, what remains uncertain, and what they would check. Measure task time, correctness against the answer key, missing caveats, unnecessary follow-up questions, and confidence in the answers. Wrong confidence is a failure even when reading is faster. An AI paraphrase or readability score is not a human outcome.

Record every failure, the sample's limits, and whether people voluntarily use the skill again. Expand only if it helps across several cases without increasing material omissions. If it merely sounds nicer, change the workflow rather than the slogan.
