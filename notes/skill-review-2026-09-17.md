# Using and reviewing Humane

## Practical feedback

The strongest behavior is preserving distinctions: a proposed event does not become confirmed, two differently labelled notices do not automatically form a timeline, a warm message can still make an ending clear, and the skill does not treat repeated claims as evidence. The source links make a brief easier to check rather than simply easier to believe.

The main usability risk is ceremony. If the person asks for two bullets, a coverage footer is not an extra favor. If they ask for an everyday explanation, requesting source documents would make the skill harder than ordinary chat. More rules are not automatically more value.

## How it was used

Before editing, nine separate trials covered eight new situations and a real use of the project's own introduction: explain to a nontechnical friend how to try Humane in chat in fewer than 90 words. The latter correctly separated copying the instructions and attaching authorized material from the unverified upload interface and unmeasured human benefits. It did not pretend to install the skill.

The eight new fictional situations were personal closure, exactly two bullets, same-date “final” notices, a missing cited charter, a private organiser note used for a public group message, a general password-manager explanation, a one-sentence tea invitation, and a source-only question about a decline in seedling counts.

No material violation was observed in those exploratory responses. The edits below are clarifications of real ambiguities in the written instructions, not fixes for failures that were never observed.

## What changed

- Ask for source material only when the requested task depends on it. A general explanation or invitation should not trigger a questionnaire.
- Distinguish general-knowledge explanation from source-only review. Do not use ordinary knowledge to invent a cause missing from a report.
- Treat a referenced-but-unsupplied document as a material coverage gap when the answer depends on it.
- Fit necessary references and limitations into the requested output shape; do not add a footer to “exactly two bullets.”

The skill remains one Markdown workflow, with no new mode, command vocabulary, profile, runtime, or configuration. It grew by about 90 words rather than gaining another subsystem.

## Retest and stopping point

After the single grouped revision, all 24 cases were exercised separately: the original 16 plus the eight additions. [The final captured responses](smoke-results-2026-09-17.json) identify the exact skill fingerprint. The implementing assistant found no material violation of the case criteria in those responses. [Earlier evidence](smoke-results.json), including the first day's failures, remains unchanged.

The loop stops here because the agreed scope is covered and another unstructured self-review would not establish perfection. These are single-response, same-family model checks, not blinded tests, cross-model reliability, real parser tests, or evidence that humans understand faster. The next useful evidence is a small real-reader comparison using the [existing pilot plan](design.md#small-human-reader-pilot), not another promise that the skill is perfect.
